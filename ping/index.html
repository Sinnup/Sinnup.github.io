Ping.

Ping is an Android app that automatically answers Marketplace buyers who ask whether an item is still available. It works for any Marketplace seller — personal or business account — with no registered Meta business profile, no API and no server. Install it on the phone that is signed in to the seller's Messenger, grant one permission, and it is ready.

Platform Android
Channels Messenger · WhatsApp Business
Setup One permission, no server

Why

Close sales sooner, without chasing anyone

  • Reply while the buyer is still interested
    Ping was built to close sales as soon as possible: the buyer gets an answer and a proposal to arrange the delivery right away, without the seller being intrusive or insistent.
  • Handles the repetitive part
    People ask "is it still available?" all the time, but many are just browsing. Ping takes care of that repetitive first reply so the seller can focus on the buyers who actually follow up.

How it works

No API, no server

  • Reads notifications, not the Meta API
    A background notification listener watches Messenger and WhatsApp Business. When an incoming message contains "disponible", Ping answers through the notification's own inline-reply action — so it needs no business profile, tokens or backend.
  • Replies that feel human
    Each reply is picked at random from several wordings, addressed by the buyer's first name and sent after a short typing delay. Users can also provide their own messages.
  • Schedule-aware
    Variants promising delivery "today or tomorrow" are only used when both days are operating days and it is before the same-day cutoff; otherwise a generic reply is sent.
  • Never double-replies, recovers missed messages
    A persisted record of delivered replies prevents duplicates, and a reconnect catch-up answers messages that arrived while the OS had the listener killed.
  • Privacy-safe
    Message text and buyer names never leave the phone; telemetry only carries counts, booleans and channel labels. Because inline-reply automation is outside Google Play policy, Ping is for personal use and is not published on the Play Store.
