Papalote.

AI-powered no-code app builder for Android. Describe any app in plain words — a habit tracker, a Pomodoro timer, a budget calculator — and Papalote builds a real, fully interactive one on your phone in seconds. No coding, no templates, no cloud accounts required.

Papalote Features

Let your creativity fly... Create what you need, right away

01

One sentence, one app

Papalote opens with a single idea: describe what you want and get a real, working app. Quick shortcuts — habits, tips, expenses, notes — get you started in one tap.

02

Describe your idea

The floating chat opens when you need it. Write in your own words what app you want — no templates, no code.

03

Name it your way

Every app is saved automatically. Rename it any time, e.g. “Punto de venta La estación del Antojo”.

04

Your app library

All your creations in one place, with a counter in the bar. Switch between them in one tap, or duplicate, share, rename and delete them.

05

Duplicate as a template

Want to try a change without risking your app? Make a copy in one tap and experiment with a variant.

06

Add to your home screen

Pin any app to your Android home screen and open it like any other app on your phone.

07

Delete with confirmation

A clear prompt keeps you from losing an app with a stray tap. You decide whether it goes.

08

Share your app as a project

Send your app via WhatsApp, Telegram, Drive or email as a Papalote project file. Whoever receives it can import it and keep editing.

09

Share it as HTML

Recipient doesn't have Papalote? Export a single HTML file that opens in any browser, offline, with your data included.

10

Your opinion counts

In-app feedback: thumbs up or down, an optional note, and the history of what you've already sent.

11

Everything at hand

One menu for the essentials: appearance, share, export and import projects, fix the current app, start from scratch, run in background and send feedback.

12

Your app, full screen

The generated app takes over the whole screen and the chat folds into a discreet bar. Here, a point of sale with search, categories, cart and bottom navigation.

13

Theme and color, your way

Pick Auto, Light or Dark mode and an accent color for each app. Change the look without regenerating anything.

14

As detailed as you want

Write long prompts with rules, scores and even phone sensors. Here, a charades game with players, topics, round timer and final ranking.

15

Watch it progress

A live panel shows which stage your app is in — reading your idea, building it, reviewing it, applying the theme — with a timer in view.

16

If it takes a while, it tells you

Complex designs take a bit longer. Papalote explains it with a clear message while it keeps working.

17

Builds in the background

Leave the app or lock the screen: a notification tracks progress and generation keeps going on its own.

18

Review and polish

After building, a reviewer agent checks the design and “polishes the details” before saving, so the app comes out ready to use.

19

Your app, ready to use

The result: “Charadas en grupo”, with player, topic and word setup, turn duration and rounds, plus section navigation.

20

Another idea, another app

Papalote understands English and Spanish. A short sentence like “Create a tip calculator…” is enough to get started.

21

Works from the first tap

Number pad, tip percentages, history and settings: the calculator works from the first screen, and everything is editable.

Architecture

How it's built

  • 🤖
    Koog AI Agent
    A Koog AIAgent runs a multi-role pipeline (Architect → Builder → Reviewer → Fixer → Finalizer). A deterministic HtmlValidator enforces hard invariants (shell, offline, no-gradient) with zero extra tokens — violations trigger a single targeted repair call, never a loop.
  • ⚙️
    Background-safe foreground service
    GenerationService is a started foreground service that survives screen lock on aggressive OEM power managers (OnePlus, Oppo, Xiaomi). A withContext(NonCancellable) finalize block guarantees delivery even if the service tears down mid-run. The real fix on aggressive OEMs is the "Allow background activity" per-app toggle — the app surfaces a one-time prompt for it.
  • 🎨
    Locally bundled design system
    Generated apps use a Material 3 + Tailwind system served from assets/web/ via WebViewAssetLoader — fully offline. Light/dark and accent are stamped via data-theme/data-accent attributes on <html> at generation time and re-stamped live when the system theme changes.
  • 💾
    Per-app state persistence
    A @JavascriptInterface bridge wraps the generated app's render() to push JSON.stringify(state) to native on every change. State is restored via Object.assign(state, saved); render() on page load — surviving app switches and process restarts.
  • 📊
    Privacy-first analytics
    21 typed events via a backend-agnostic Analytics interface backed by Firebase. AnalyticsSchema (pure, no Android deps) is the single source of truth — unit-tested to enforce that payloads carry only lengths/counts/flags/enums, never prompt or HTML text.
